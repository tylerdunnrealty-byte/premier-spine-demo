Step 1 of 4 · Understand

01 / A LITTLE CONTEXT

Meet your neck.

Your neck is the upper part of your spine, also called the cervical spine. Start with three useful building blocks.

01

Vertebrae

Seven small bones form the neck portion of your spine.

02

Discs

Flexible cushions between the bones help absorb everyday movement.

03

Nerves

Nerve roots branch from the spinal cord through openings in the bones and carry messages between your brain and body.

You do not need to identify what is causing your symptoms here. That conversation belongs with your clinician.
