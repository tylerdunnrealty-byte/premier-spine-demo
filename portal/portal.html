About this demo

Demo — example data only. This is a prototype of a patient portal for Premier Spine. Every person, appointment, document and message is made up. Do not enter real health information. It is not production and not HIPAA-compliant.

In this online demo

  • The screens are recordings of the working prototype, made with fictional patients.
  • Nothing you type here is sent or saved. Buttons that would save say so.
  • In the full prototype, answers, messages and call-back requests are stored by the server and the office sees them as tasks.

Simulated (looks real, is not)

  • Faxes to and from other offices, text messages and their delivery receipts.
  • Insurance checks and approvals, and automatic follow-ups.
  • “Ask” answers come from a short list of saved office answers — not a person, a clinician or medical triage.
  • Sign-in has no password. There is no scheduling system: we can’t show open appointment times.

Not built

  • Real identity checks, real messaging, real records, office hours and after-hours routing, and medical triage.
  • Wording about symptoms and surgery is example text waiting for Dr. Yakel’s review.
