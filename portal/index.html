      Built for Premier

      Your portal, not a rented one

      Your brand

      Premier Spine’s name, colours, phone number and care team on every screen.

      Your workflow

      Referrals, outside records, prior authorizations, intake, surgery prep and post-op check-ins follow the steps your office already uses.

      Your rules

      The practice sets the escalation rules, the approved instructions and the reminder timing. The portal doesn’t write medical advice.

      What a real build needs: a signed BAA, a HIPAA security review, a secure host and integration with your EHR and scheduling. None of that is in place in this demo.

      About this demo

      • Every patient, staff member, document and message is made up. Please don’t enter real health information.
      • The screens are recordings of the working prototype. Nothing you type here is sent or saved, and buttons that would save say so.
      • Faxes, text messages, insurer replies and follow-ups are simulated. The assistant is scripted; no AI model is connected.
      • No tracking, no analytics and no cookies; the only thing kept in your browser is the “Larger text” setting. Not production and not HIPAA-compliant.
